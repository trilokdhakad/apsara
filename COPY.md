# Voice

Clear.
Confident.
Understated.
Technical when useful.

Avoid:

revolutionary
game-changing
next-generation
seamless
cutting-edge
AI-powered everything
10x
unlock
supercharge


## Claims

Never convert a vision into a present-tense product claim.

Bad:
"We analyze every counselling seat in real time."

Good:
"We're building a system that can continuously analyze counselling changes."

Bad:
"Get the college you want."

Good:
"Build a strategy around the colleges you want."

Do not imply guaranteed admission.

Do not use unsupported numerical claims.

Do not use "AI" merely as decoration. Mention it only when it adds useful information.
